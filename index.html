About the Lab

Where AI Meets
the Mining Industry

The Mine Digitalization & Artificial Intelligence (MDAI) Lab operates within the Department of Mining Engineering at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad — India's oldest and most prestigious mining institute, established in 1926.

Under the leadership of Dr. Siddhartha Agarwal, and sponsored by Mahanadi Coalfields Limited (MCL), we develop intelligent systems that protect miners, predict hazards, automate inspections, and digitize mine operations end-to-end.

🧠

AI & Machine Learning

NLP, RAG systems, transformer models, and deep learning tailored for mining domain data

📡

IoT & Digital Twins

Real-time sensor networks, drone surveys, and digital simulation of mine environments

⛑️

Mine Safety Systems

Data-driven accident analysis, automated inspection, and hazard prediction at scale

🤝

Industry Collaboration

Working with Coal India, MCL, SCCL and regulatory bodies to deploy production-grade tools

Tools Built by Our Lab

Our Products & Applications

Real tools, real impact — built by our students, project staffs, PhD scholars, and interns for the mining industry.

Web App · AI Analytics

⚒️ Shield Mine

Mining Accident Analysis & RAG System platform with Hybrid RAG (BM25 + Vector search), automated safety audit report generation, chi-squared association analysis, K-means clustering, and interactive visualizations across 5,000+ incident records.

🛠 Lab Team · 2024 · Streamlit · LangChain · Groq LLM
⚒ Launch Shield Mine ↗
AI Inspection · Health & Safety

🛡️ AIMSURE

AI-powered Mine Inspection & Safety Management platform designed to streamline health, safety, and compliance monitoring. Features intelligent inspection workflows, automated safety assessments, worker health tracking, regulatory compliance analysis, real-time reporting dashboards, and AI-driven insights for safer mining operations.

🛠 Lab Team · 2026 · Flutter · Dart · AI · Figma
🛡 Launch AIMSURE ↗
AI Powered · Tree Intelligence

🌲 Forestlens AI

AI-powered Forest Intelligence & GIS platform designed to automate canopy analysis, species identification, and tree height estimation. Features intelligent satellite and drone imagery processing, automated tree enumeration workflows, biodiversity tracking, real-time height and biomass modeling, and spatial analytics dashboards for sustainable forestry management.

🛠 Lab Team · 2026 · GIS · Computer Vision · ML · PyTorch
